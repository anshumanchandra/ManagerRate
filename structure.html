ManagerRate is a fully anonymous platform where employees can share honest feedback about their managers — driving accountability, transparency, and better leadership across every company.

Why We Built This

Great organizations are built on great leadership. Yet most companies lack a safe, anonymous channel for employees to share candid feedback about their managers. ManagerRate was created to fill that gap — giving every team member a voice without fear of retaliation, and giving leaders the insight they need to genuinely grow.

How It Changes the World

When managers receive honest, constructive feedback, cultures transform. Teams become more engaged, turnover drops, and innovation flourishes. By making leadership accountability the norm rather than the exception, ManagerRate helps create workplaces where people genuinely thrive — one review at a time.

Built for Organizations That Care

ManagerRate isn't just for employees — it's a powerful hiring and culture tool for organizations that refuse to compromise on work environment. Here's why forward-thinking companies are paying attention.

Hire the Right Managers, Not Just the Right Resumes

A candidate's resume tells you where they worked and what they shipped — but it says nothing about how they led. Did they inspire their team or burn them out? Did people grow under them or quietly leave? ManagerRate gives your hiring committee something no interview can: the unfiltered truth from the people who reported to them. Before you hand someone the keys to a team of 15, check their leadership track record — the way you'd check references, but better.

Due Diligence on Leadership Hires

Search any managerial candidate by name or LinkedIn profile. See their ratings across every company they've managed at — not just the story they tell in an interview.

  • Track record that follows them across companies
  • Red flags surface before the offer letter

  • Reduce the risk of culture-destroying hires
