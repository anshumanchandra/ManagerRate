Frequently Asked Questions

How are reviews verified as authentic?
Every review requires a valid LinkedIn profile URL of the manager being reviewed. We use rate limiting, IP-based duplicate detection, and content moderation to prevent spam. Reviews with 3+ community reports are automatically flagged for review.
Can managers see who wrote their reviews?
Absolutely not. ManagerRate is designed with privacy-by-design architecture. We never store reviewer identities — not even hashed. Our database literally cannot link a review to a person. Even with full database access, it's impossible to identify reviewers.
What's included in a Leadership Report?
Each report includes: overall score and percentile ranking, detailed breakdown across all 11 leadership categories, strengths and improvement areas, sentiment analysis of review text, rating trend over time, company-by-company performance, recommendation rate, red flag detection, and peer benchmarking against industry averages.
How does the Company Dashboard work?
The dashboard gives you a real-time view of all managers reviewed at your company. You'll see aggregated ratings, trends over time, automated alerts for declining managers, and exportable data. It's like having a pulse check on your entire leadership team.
Can I try Pro features before committing?
Yes! We offer a 14-day free trial of the Starter plan. No credit card required. You'll get full access to Leadership Reports, the comparison tool, and API access during the trial.
Is there an Enterprise plan for large organizations?
Yes. For organizations with 200+ managers or custom requirements, we offer tailored Enterprise plans with SSO integration, custom categories, dedicated support, SLA guarantees, and volume pricing. Contact our sales team to discuss.
What security measures protect the data?
We use 16+ layers of security including: DOMPurify XSS prevention, rate limiting, Content Security Policy, HTTPS-only, encrypted databases, hashed API keys, non-root Docker containers, and automated content moderation. Our full security audit is publicly available.
Can I cancel my subscription anytime?
Yes, you can cancel anytime. Monthly plans cancel at the end of the billing period. Annual plans can be cancelled with a prorated refund for unused months. No lock-in, no penalties.
